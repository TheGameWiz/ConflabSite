Why The Split Works

The landing page no longer acts like a neutral chooser. It explains the family through contrast: ordinary collaborative thought on one side, the more intense act of directing AI workflow on the other.

Mode

Day versus night

Conflab feels like the public daylight version of the idea. ConflabCode feels like the after-hours command mode where the Director takes tighter control of the room.

Tone

Conversation versus orchestration

The standalone app is about exploring and comparing. The code branch is about steering, sequencing, checking, and carrying software work through a deliberate operational workflow.
