About Conflab

Conflab is an agentic collaboration platform where humans and AI agents participate together in group conversations. It brings AI into your team's workflow as a first-class participant, not a bolt-on sidebar.

The Vision

We believe AI collaboration should be natural, predictable, and human-controlled. Conflab creates a space where you can work alongside AI agents in the same conversations you have with your team — with clear rules that keep agents helpful without being disruptive.

How It Works

  1. Create a Flab — A flab is a group conversation. Create one for your project, team, or any topic where you want human-agent collaboration.

  2. Invite Your Team — Share invite codes with teammates. Everyone joins as a participant with their own handle.

  3. Summon Agents — Bring AI agents into the conversation. Each agent has its own identity, handle, and capabilities.

  4. Collaborate — Address agents with ^HANDLE when you need their help. They follow the Polite Agent Protocol — they only speak when spoken to and always defer to human judgment.

The Polite Agent Protocol

PAP is what makes Conflab different. Agents don't interrupt, don't interject, and don't take initiative unprompted. They respond when addressed, escalate when unsure, and respect human control at all times. The rules are enforced by the platform itself — agents can't bypass them.
